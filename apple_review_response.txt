===================================================================
1. NATIVE INTERACTIVE TACTICAL UTILITIES & SIMULATORS (Tools Tab)
===================================================================
We have developed a suite of fully interactive native tools and calculators built specifically for GTA 6 players:

• Heist Payout & Crew Split Calculator:
  - An interactive financial simulation tool allowing players to adjust heist score amounts ($250,000 to $20,000,000) using dynamic sliders.
  - Choose between tactical approaches (e.g., Smart Hacker, Aggressive Assault, Stealth Breach).
  - Customize cut percentages for each crew member (Lucia, Jason, Hacker, Driver, Gunman, Fencing fee) with real-time recalculation of net takes.

• Vehicle Garage & Side-by-Side Comparator:
  - An interactive vehicle benchmarking utility allowing players to select any two vehicles from the database and compare their real-time performance telemetry.
  - Live animated comparison bars for Top Speed, 0-60 Acceleration, Braking, Handling, Armor, and In-Game Price.

• Weapon Arsenal & Ballistics TTK Simulator:
  - An interactive ballistics calculator that computes Shots-To-Kill and Time-To-Kill (TTK) across variable engagement ranges (5m to 100m) and body target zones (Head, Torso, Limbs).
  - Inspect fire rates, magazine capacities, and attachment setups.

• 100% Leonida Completion Tracker:
  - An interactive multi-category completion checklist covering Story Missions, Strangers & Freaks, Hobbies, Random Encounters, and Collectibles.
  - Live progress indicators, radial progress rings, and automatic local data persistence.

• Vice City Lore Trivia & Quiz Game:
  - A timed interactive trivia mini-game testing game lore and mechanics.
  - Features real-time scoring, tactile haptic feedback, detailed answer explanations, and local high-score tracking.

===================================================================
2. INTERACTIVE WALKTHROUGH GUIDES WITH PROGRESS CHECKLISTS (Guides Tab)
===================================================================
• Interactive Step-by-Step Checklists:
  - Guides now include actionable checklists that players can check off in real-time as they complete objectives during gameplay.
  - Visual progress bars update automatically per guide and across overall progress.

• In-App Player Notes Scratchpad:
  - Every guide features a dedicated personal notes editor allowing players to record custom strategies, loadouts, and heist plans, stored locally via UserDefaults.

• Native Categorization & Bookmarks:
  - Multi-category filters (Missions & Heists, Money & Business, Collectibles, Weapons, Secrets) and a 1-tap bookmarking system.

===================================================================
3. VISUAL CHEAT VAULT WITH NATIVE CONTROLLER BUTTONS (Cheats Tab)
===================================================================
• Dual Platform Controller Visualizer:
  - Renders authentic PlayStation 5 (DualSense) and Xbox Series X|S controller button sequences using dedicated image assets (△, ○, ✕, □, bumpers, triggers, D-pad directionals).
  - Supports PC key commands and in-game phone number dials.

• 1-Tap Copy with Haptic Feedback:
  - Instant clipboard copying powered by native iOS UIImpactFeedbackGenerator and UINotificationFeedbackGenerator for tactile confirmation.

• Detailed Cheat Breakdown:
  - Dedicated detail view with warning notices regarding achievement/trophy disabling, usage tips, and effect summaries.
